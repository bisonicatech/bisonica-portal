Clocka beta · tester briefing

Thanks for testing Clocka

Clocka is a gentle day clock for someone living with dementia. It shows the time, whether it's morning or night, and one simple thing to do now. Their family can help from their own phones.

Time: about 30 minutes, plus one check-in later You need: 2 phones (yours and a partner's) Cost: free, including Premium

Before you start

  • There are no accounts or passwords. You set Clocka up once on each phone and link them with a short code.
  • Use made-up names, such as "Grandad", and don't enter anyone's real medical details. This is test data.
  • You'll need a partner and two phones: one becomes the clock for the person being cared for, the other the family phone. Team up with someone at home or a friend. iPhone and Android can be mixed. On your own? Message Jason and he'll be your family phone.
  • Something not working is a useful result. Please tell us about it.
Step 1

Install the test app

iPhone via TestFlight

  1. Install Apple's free TestFlight app from the App Store.
  2. Open the invite link Jason sends you (or the email from TestFlight) on your iPhone.
  3. Tap Accept, then Install.
  4. Open Clocka. When asked, tap Allow for notifications.

Needs iOS 16 or later. Updates appear in TestFlight; tap Update when you see one.

Android via download link

  1. Open the download link Jason sends you, on your Android phone.
  2. Tap Install. If Android asks, allow "Install unknown apps" for your browser. This is normal for test apps.
  3. Open Clocka. When asked, tap Allow for notifications.
  4. Recommended: Settings → Apps → Clocka → Battery → Unrestricted, so reminders aren't delayed.

Needs Android 10 or later.

Step 2

Set up the two phones

Do the family phone first, because it makes the code that links the clock.

Phone A · the family phone
  1. Tap A family member or carer.
  2. Tap Set up for the first time.
  3. Type a made-up name for the person you care for, your name, and your real phone number (for their Call button).
  4. Tap Continue. You'll see an 8-digit link code, like 4180 8460.
Phone B · the clock
  1. Tap The person being cared for.
  2. Type the same made-up name.
  3. Type the 8-digit code from Phone A.
  4. Choose a 4-digit settings PIN and tap Start.

The code works once and lasts 15 minutes. If it runs out, tap Get a new code on Phone A.

Step 3

Try these, in order

Make a quick note of anything that's confusing, slow, cut off or broken. A screenshot helps.

  1. Read the clock Both

    On Phone B: can you read the time, the day and the part of the day (morning, afternoon…) from arm's length? Does the message make sense?

  2. Check the link Both

    On Phone A: does it say "✓ … phone is connected", and show what the clock is saying right now?

  3. Change the message from the family phone Both

    Phone A → Edit … daily schedule. Change the message for the current time, then Save.

    Expected: Phone B shows the new message within about 15 seconds.

  4. Get a reminder sound Both

    In the schedule, tap + Add a time. Set it 3 minutes from now, type a message, leave Reminder sound on, and Save. Lock Phone B and wait.

    Expected: Phone B makes a sound and shows a notification at that time.

  5. Try night-time Both

    Add another time 2 minutes from now and set it to Night (silent).

    Expected: Phone B turns dark, says it's night-time, makes no sound, and hides the Call button. Delete these two test times afterwards.

  6. Go out Both

    Phone A → Going out? Type a "back by" time, choose 30 min check-ins, tap Start Out mode.

    Expected: Phone B shows where you've gone and when you'll be back.

  7. Answer a check-in iPhone

    After about 30 minutes, Phone B asks "Are you okay?" Tap I'm okay 👍. Phone A should show the time of the last "I'm okay".

    Extra: next time, ignore it. About 15 minutes later Phone A should get a "hasn't answered" alert. Then tap I'm home on Phone A.

  8. Use the Call button Both

    On Phone B, tap Call …. Phone A's number should ring. Hang up.

  9. Open the clock's settings Both

    On Phone B, press and hold the time for 3 seconds, then type your PIN. Change something small and Save.

  10. Press the back button Android

    On the clock screen, back should do nothing. In settings or help pages, it should go back one step.

  11. Try Premium, free iPhone

    Phone A → Try Premium free for a month, then choose a plan. You won't be charged, because test purchases are free. Apple may show "[Sandbox]".

    Expected: Premium switches on within a few seconds on both phones. Try different times on different days and Send a message to their screen.

  12. Look at Help and Privacy Both

    Open Help and support and What Clocka keeps and why. Is anything unclear?

  13. Finish: delete the test data Both

    When you're done, on Phone A tap Delete all of … Clocka data… and confirm. Both phones should go back to the start screen.

Known gaps in this test version

  • Android "Are you okay?" check-ins and missed alerts don't arrive yet, and Premium isn't available on Android yet.
  • iPhone The FaceTime video button is iPhone only.
  • No need to report these, unless something else goes wrong around them.
Step 4

Send your feedback

Fill in the short feedback form. The link is in Jason's message, and it takes about 5 minutes. Please send it within a week of installing.

Screenshots or screen recordings are really helpful. Email them to info@clocka.co.uk with your name and phone model.

Stuck?

  • The code doesn't work: codes last 15 minutes and work once. Get a new one on Phone A. After 10 wrong tries, wait 15 minutes.
  • No sound: check notifications are allowed for Clocka, the volume is up and the phone isn't on silent.
  • Start again: in settings, tap Change what this phone is for…, or delete and reinstall the app.
  • Anything else: message Jason, or email info@clocka.co.uk.

Thank you. Every bit of feedback helps families living with dementia.
