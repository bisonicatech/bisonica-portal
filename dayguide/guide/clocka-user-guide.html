Words used in this guide

The cared-for person is the person living with dementia who uses the Clocka clock on their phone.

The family member is the partner, son, daughter, friend or carer who sets Clocka up and looks after it from their own phone. In the examples, David is the cared-for person and Carrie is his wife.

Linked means the two phones are connected, so a change on one shows on the other.

Important

Clocka is a daily routine and reminder app. It is not a medical device and it does not monitor anyone's health or safety. It does not replace care, supervision or emergency services. In an emergency, call 999.

Everyone
Section 1

What Clocka does

Clocka answers the question many people with dementia ask again and again: what should I be doing now?

Clocka runs on two phones:

The cared-for person's phone shows the clock

  • The time, the day and the date, in big clear letters.
  • Whether it's morning, afternoon, evening or night-time.
  • One simple message for right now, like "Time for lunch".
  • A big button to call a family member.
  • At night it's dark and silent: "It's still night-time. Go back to bed."

The family member's phone looks after it

  • Plan the messages for each part of the day.
  • See what the clock is saying right now.
  • Out mode: when you go out, the clock says where you've gone and when you'll be back.
  • Check-ins: while you're out, Clocka asks "Are you okay?" and tells you if there's no answer.
Good to know

The clock works even without the family phone. If you only want the clock, you can set up the cared-for person's phone on its own and leave the link code empty. You can link the phones later.

Section 2

Before you start

You will need

  • Two smartphones: the cared-for person's phone and your own. Clocka is available for iPhone, and Android is coming soon.
  • An internet connection on both phones (Wi-Fi or mobile data) so they can stay linked.
  • About 10 minutes, with both phones in front of you.
  • A charger for the cared-for person's phone, ideally by their bed.

Install Clocka on both phones

On each iPhone, open the App Store, search for Clocka and tap Get. You'll find a link at clocka.co.uk too.

Allow notifications

The first time Clocka starts, the phone asks whether Clocka may send notifications. Tap Allow on both phones.

  • On the cared-for person's phone, notifications give the gentle reminder sounds and the "Are you okay?" check-ins.
  • On your phone, they tell you if a check-in is missed while you're out.
If you tapped "Don't Allow"

Clocka shows a yellow card saying Notifications are switched off. Tap Open Settings, then Notifications, and turn on Allow Notifications.

Choose a 4-digit PIN

The settings on the cared-for person's phone are protected by a 4-digit PIN, so the clock can't be changed by accident. Choose a number you'll remember, but not one the cared-for person will guess. If you don't choose one, the PIN is 1234.

Section 3

Setting up the family member's phone

Start with your own phone. It makes a short code that links the cared-for person's phone.

  1. Open Clocka. Under Who will use this phone?, tap A family member or carer.
  2. Tap Set up for the first time.
  3. Type the name of the person you care for. Use the name they know best, like "David" or "Dad". It appears in messages such as "Good morning, David!"
  4. Type your name and your phone number. The number is for the Call button on their clock.
  5. Tap Continue.

Clocka now shows a 8-digit link code. Keep this screen open and pick up the cared-for person's phone (section 4).

Each code works once and lasts 15 minutes. If it runs out, tap Get a new code. You can always find it later under Show a link code… on your phone.

Tip

The link code is not the settings PIN. The link code connects the phones. The PIN protects the settings on the cared-for person's phone.

Section 4

Setting up the cared-for person's phone

  1. Open Clocka on their phone. Tap The person being cared for.
  2. Type their first name.
  3. Under Link to a family member's phone, type the 8-digit link code from your phone.
  4. Choose a 4-digit settings PIN.
  5. Tap Start.

The clock appears. On your phone, Clocka now says "✓ David's phone is connected".

When you link the phones, the Call button uses the name and number you typed on your phone. If you don't link the phones, you can type a name and number here instead.

Tips for this phone
  • Keep it charging by the bed at night, so the clock is there when they wake.
  • Allow notifications when asked.
  • Turn off Low Power Mode, which can delay reminders.
  • Leave Clocka open. While it's open, the screen stays on during the day. At night the phone can switch its screen off as normal.
If the code doesn't work

Check the 8 digits and try again, or tap Get a new code on your phone. After 10 wrong codes, Clocka asks you to wait 15 minutes. This stops strangers guessing codes.

At night

From bedtime until morning, the screen is dark and Clocka makes no sounds at all. If they wake at 3am, they see "It's still night-time. Everyone is asleep. Go back to bed." The Call button is hidden at night, so they're not tempted to call at 3am.

Section 6

Planning the day

Clocka starts with a ready-made day: get up at 7:00, breakfast, a cup of tea, lunch, dinner, get ready for bed at 9pm and night-time from 10pm. Change it to fit their routine.

  1. On your phone, tap Edit David's daily schedule.
  2. For each time, set:
    • the time it starts, such as 07:30 or 21:00
    • the message, such as "Time for lunch"
    • a picture: tap the picture to choose another
    • the part of the day: Morning, Day, Evening or Night (silent)
    • whether to play a reminder sound at that time
  3. Tap + Add a time for more, or Delete to remove one.
  4. Tap Save.

Each message stays on the screen until the next time starts. Changes reach their phone within about 15 seconds while Clocka is open on it.

Free plan: up to 8 times a day, the same every day. Premium: unlimited times, and a different plan for each day of the week. For example, "Sara is coming today" only on Saturdays.

Writing messages that help
  • Short and simple: one thing at a time. "Time for lunch" works better than a list.
  • Say what to do, not what not to do: "Go back to bed" rather than "Don't get up".
  • Use their words: "cuppa", "the paper", their dog's name.
  • Be warm: type {name} straight after a word and Clocka puts their name in. "Good morning{name}!" shows as "Good morning, David!"
  • Mark night times Night (silent), so nothing ever makes a sound at night.

Section 7

Going out: Out mode and check-ins

Popping to the shops? Out mode tells them where you are, and check-ins tell you they're okay.

Starting Out mode

  1. On your phone, scroll to Going out?
  2. Type when you'll be back by, such as "3pm" or "15:30".
  3. Choose how often to ask "Are you okay?": every 30 minutes, 1 hour or 2 hours.
  4. Check the message they'll see, then tap Start Out mode.

How check-ins work

  1. At each check-in time, their phone shows "Are you okay, David?" and the green I'm okay 👍 button appears on the clock.
  2. When they tap it, your phone shows the time of the last "I'm okay".
  3. If there's no answer within 15 minutes, your phone gets an alert: "David hasn't answered the check-in". It's a good moment to give them a call.

Coming home

Tap I'm home and the clock goes back to the normal day. If you're more than 15 minutes late, the clock stops showing your "back at" time. It says "Carrie will be home soon. Call Carrie if you need anything." instead.

Please remember

Check-ins need both phones to be switched on, with notifications and internet. A missed check-in may simply mean they didn't hear the phone. Clocka can't tell you why someone hasn't answered, and it doesn't replace care or supervision. Only the family member who set Clocka up receives missed check-in alerts.

More family members and Premium

Adding another family member (Premium)

Brothers, sisters, children and friends can all follow along on their own phones. They can see what the clock says, edit the schedule and turn on Out mode.

  1. On your phone, tap Show a link code…
  2. On their phone, open Clocka, tap A family member or carer, then I have a code.
  3. They type their name and the 8-digit code, then tap Continue.

Section 9

Settings on the cared-for person's phone

There are no buttons for settings on the clock, so it can't be changed by accident.

  1. Press and hold the time on the clock for 3 seconds.
  2. Type the 4-digit PIN.

In Family settings you can:

  • link to a family member's phone, or link again with a new code
  • change their name, the Call button's name and number, and the PIN
  • edit the daily schedule (it's easier from your own phone)
  • read Help and support and the privacy summary
  • remove Clocka from this phone

Tap Save when you've finished, or Cancel to leave things as they were.

Forgotten the PIN?

Uninstall Clocka from the phone and install it again, then link it with a new code. If it was linked, the daily plan comes back from the family phone.

Section 10

Getting the best from Clocka

Introducing Clocka

  • Show them the clock together, while they're calm. Read the message aloud: "It says it's time for a cup of tea."
  • Point to the clock when they ask what to do: "Let's see what Clocka says."
  • Give it a week or two. Routines take time to become familiar.
  • Print the large-print card at the end of this guide and keep it by the phone.

Keep the phone ready

  • Keep it on charge by the bed at night, and somewhere they'll see it during the day.
  • Keep Clocka open on the screen. Swiping away from it is easy, so consider Guided Access (below).
  • Make sure the volume is up and the ring/silent switch is off if you want reminder sounds.
  • Leave Low Power Mode off.
Tip: keep the phone on Clocka with Guided Access (iPhone)

Guided Access is part of the iPhone. It keeps the phone in one app until you enter a passcode.

  1. Open iPhone Settings, then Accessibility, then Guided Access, and turn it on. Set a passcode.
  2. Open Clocka, then quickly press the side button three times and tap Start.
  3. To finish, press the side button three times and enter the passcode.

With Guided Access on, notification banners may not appear. Check-ins still work, because the I'm okay button appears on the clock itself. Try a day with it on to check the reminder sounds suit you.

Check the screen from your phone

The family screen shows "Right now David's screen says…" and whether their phone is connected. If it says the phone hasn't connected for a while, it may be switched off, out of charge, offline, or Clocka may be closed.

Questions and problems

My changes aren't showing on their phone.

Clocka needs to be open on their phone, with internet. Changes usually arrive within 15 seconds. Check your phone says "✓ David's phone is connected".

There are no reminder sounds.

Check notifications are allowed, the volume is up, the ring/silent switch isn't on silent, and Reminder sound at this time is on for that time. Night times never make a sound.

I didn't get a missed check-in alert.

Alerts go to the family member who set Clocka up. Check that notifications are on for Clocka on your phone and that both phones have internet.

The link code doesn't work.

Codes work once and last 15 minutes. Tap Get a new code. After 10 wrong tries, wait 15 minutes.

The clock says it's morning, but it's still dark outside.

Edit the schedule so that "Morning" starts later, or add a later night-time message, such as one at 5:00 saying "It's still night-time. Go back to bed."

They keep closing the app.

Use Guided Access (section 10) to keep the phone on Clocka.

Does the clock work without internet?

Yes. The clock, messages and reminder sounds keep working. Linking, Out mode, check-ins and changes from the family phone need internet.

They have a new phone.

Install Clocka on the new phone, choose The person being cared for, and link it with a new code from your phone. The daily plan comes across.

I want to set up Clocka again from the start.

In settings, tap Change what this phone is for…, or remove Clocka from the phone and install it again.

Can more than one person look after the clock?

Yes, with Premium. See section 8.

Section 12

Privacy, your data and getting help

Your privacy

  • Clocka has no adverts, does no tracking and never sells data.
  • It keeps only what it needs: first names, the phone numbers for the Call buttons, the daily plan, and Out mode and check-in times. These are stored on a secure server in London so the phones can share them.
  • On the family screen, tap What Clocka keeps and why for a summary. The full privacy policy is at clocka.co.uk/privacy.

Deleting your data

  • The family member who set Clocka up can tap Delete all of David's Clocka data… on the family screen. This removes everything from the server straight away and unlinks every phone.
  • Anyone else can tap Remove Clocka from this phone…. This removes Clocka from their phone only.
  • Families that don't use Clocka for 12 months are deleted automatically.

Help with Clocka

Email info@clocka.co.uk or visit clocka.co.uk/support. In the app, tap Help and support.
